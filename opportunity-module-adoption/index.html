The Story to Tell

  • Adoption remains strong & broad — Opp reaches 74.5% of active orgs (1,872 of 2,513); weekly Opp volume set sprint highs across August (~8,200–8,400 actions/wk).
  • Sep 7 is a holiday dip, not a trend — the week of Labor Day (Sep 7) fell to 612 Opp-active orgs / 440K core; prior weeks held ~660–710 actives.
  • But Opportunity retention risk is building underneath — 496 paid accounts (~$8.13M ACV) cut Opportunity usage >30% over the last 60 days, and 60 stopped using Opp entirely.
  • 99 active paid accounts are completely dark — zero core AND zero Opp in 90 days (~$0.68M ACV), including federal names (EPA-OSDBU, Dept. of Education, VA) and several renewing within 120 days.
  • Next play: route the ACV-ranked decliner & dark lists (Excel) to CS by owner; prioritize Red-health, high-ACV, near-renewal accounts for immediate outreach.
Definitions — Paid = is_trial = false. Opp action = Opportunities ViewDetails + Favorite clicks.
